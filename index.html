About us

GM Agriculture (GMA) takes its name from its founder, Gamal Mostafa. It is a family-run business built on 40 years of trading experience and lasting partnerships. We supply local, regional and international customers with premium fruit, vegetables and beef all year round.

GMA PlantationPremium citrus and grapes, including Valencia and Murcott oranges and Flame, Crimson and Autumn Royal grapes. Grown, packed and delivered from our own farms.
Animal Production & Livestock TradeOne of Egypt's largest calf-fattening farms. We import European and South American breeds and raise them to international standards, antibiotic-residue free.
Fibers & ProteinsA "Farm to Home" online delivery service for fresh produce, beef and olive oil in Cairo, Alexandria and October 6th.
